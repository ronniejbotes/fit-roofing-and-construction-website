Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical roof installation take?

Most residential roof installations are completed within 1–3 days depending on size, materials, and weather conditions. We prioritize both efficiency and precision on every project.

Do you offer free inspections?

Yes, we provide completely free, no-obligation inspections. Our team will assess your roof and give you a detailed, transparent report with recommended solutions.

What roofing materials do you recommend?

We recommend high-quality asphalt shingles, metal roofing, or tile depending on your home and budget. All materials are selected to withstand the Texas climate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. F.I.T. Roofing & Construction is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Texas, giving you complete peace of mind throughout your project.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes, we guide you through the entire insurance claims process, ensuring all damage is properly documented and your claim is handled smoothly.
